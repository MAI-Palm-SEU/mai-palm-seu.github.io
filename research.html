Research

We study autonomous intelligence for complex, open environments, organized around three linked problems: perceiving and understanding multimodal information, reasoning with knowledge, and making decisions autonomously.

Methods

Applications
Retrieval & recommendation · Scientific discovery & research assistance

Reasoning is only as reliable as the representations it starts from, and decisions are only as sound as the knowledge behind them. Once an agent acts, by searching, opening an image or reading a page, it brings back new observations and the loop starts again. Autonomy is the ability to run this loop on one's own.

01 · Perception & understanding

Multimodal understanding

How can a system turn images, video, text and behavior into representations it can use?

We learn from images, video, text and user behavior: fusing and disentangling modalities, distilling the useful parts, and staying robust when a modality is missing or labels are noisy. Much of this work began in multimodal recommendation, and it now extends to visual commonsense reasoning, video question answering, and human–object interaction detection with large vision-language models.

Multimodal representation · Disentanglement and fusion · Visual reasoning

    02 · Semantic reasoning

    Foundation models

    How can large models reason with knowledge they can trust, whether it is stored in their parameters or retrieved from outside?

    Large models know a lot, but not always the right thing. We study how a multimodal model should reconcile its internal knowledge with retrieved visual and textual evidence, and how the semantic knowledge of language models can be used to understand reviews and short texts.

    Multimodal RAG · Knowledge reconciliation · LLM-based semantic understanding

      03 · Autonomous decision-making

      Autonomous intelligence

      How can an agent plan, search, use tools and act over many steps without losing track of its goal?

      Autonomy means running the whole loop on one's own: deciding what to look for, gathering evidence, and acting on it over many steps. Current work studies context management for multimodal agentic retrieval, evidence-grounded image generation, and memory and collaboration for agents. In embodied intelligence, our vision-language-action policies won first place in the MetaFine Open Competition at CoRL 2026.

      Agentic retrieval · Memory · Multi-agent systems · Embodied manipulation

        Applications

        Retrieval and recommendation

        Where do these capabilities meet real users and real problems?

        Recommendation and retrieval are where the group's methods have been tested longest, on graph-structured interactions, multiple behavior types, cross-domain data and privacy-preserving data synthesis. Building on this, we aim to bring these capabilities to scientific discovery and research assistance.

        Graph recommendation · Multi-behavior recommendation · Cross-domain recommendation
