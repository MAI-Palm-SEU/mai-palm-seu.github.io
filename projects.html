Projects

Our projects address specific problems within the three research directions. Each project brings together related topics, publications, code, data and participating members.

Exploratory topics

Within the same three directions, we are interested in the following topics. These are exploratory interests rather than completed results; we welcome students who would like to investigate them.

  • Semantic Reasoning · Autonomous Decision-Making

    AI for Science

    Agents that read the literature, form hypotheses and run analyses, assisting scientific discovery and everyday research.

  • Autonomous Decision-Making

    Agentic reinforcement learning

    Training agents with reinforcement learning to plan, call tools and learn from feedback over long tasks.

  • Semantic Reasoning

    Multimodal reasoning

    Step-by-step reasoning over images, video and diagrams, including thinking with images.

  • Perception & Understanding · Semantic Reasoning

    Unified understanding and generation

    One model that both understands and generates images, video and text, and keeps what it generates true to fact.

  • Perception & Understanding · Autonomous Decision-Making

    World models

    Models that predict how the world changes under actions, for planning, simulation and embodied agents.

  • Autonomous Decision-Making

    Self-evolving agents

    Agents that accumulate memory, skills and experience, and keep improving after deployment.
