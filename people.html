Faculty

Group lead

Fan Liu

刘帆

Professor · Group lead

Fan Liu is a Professor and Young Chief Professor at Southeast University, with appointments in the School of Computer Science and Engineering, the School of Software and the School of Artificial Intelligence, and a doctoral supervisor. He has been selected for a national high-level young talent program and was named among the world's top 2% scientists in 2026. He received his PhD from Shandong University and was a research fellow at the National University of Singapore.

His research covers multimodal understanding, foundation models and autonomous intelligence, focusing on perception and understanding, semantic reasoning and autonomous decision-making in complex, open environments. He has published more than 40 papers in CCF-A conferences and IEEE/ACM Transactions, and two monographs as first author.

Selected honors

  • ACM China SIGMM Doctoral Dissertation Award
  • 1st Place, MetaFine Open Competition, CoRL 2026
  • 1st Place, CVPR 2026 DataMFM Challenge
  • 1st Place, SIGIR 2026 eCommerce Data Challenge
  • 1st Place, ECCV 2024 Corner Case Scene Generation Challenge
  • Outstanding Reviewer, ACM WSDM and IEEE TMM
