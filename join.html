Join us

If our research interests overlap with yours, we would be glad to hear from you. Our research framework and projects show what you would work on.

Graduate students

We are recruiting master's students and 2–3 PhD students for 2027 admission. Students from computer science, software engineering, artificial intelligence, automation, mathematics, statistics and related fields are welcome. Current openings are listed in the official faculty notice.

Research interns

We welcome undergraduate students to join our research year-round. Tell us about your interests and the time you can commit.

Postdoctoral researchers

Applicants with related research experience are welcome. Please include a CV, representative publications, and a brief research plan.

What we look for

  1. Working knowledge of at least one programming language
  2. Sustained enthusiasm and self-motivation for research
  3. A solid foundation in English and mathematics

Send a short introduction, your CV, and the research questions you are interested in.
