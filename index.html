Multimodal Autonomous Intelligence

Our research brings together multimodal understanding, foundation models and autonomous intelligence. We study how agents can make sense of complex, open environments using the full range of signals available to them, from language and vision to human behavior and feedback from their own actions. Our goal is for agents to reason from this evidence and choose how to act, whether a retrieval agent is deciding what information to seek next or an embodied agent is adjusting its grasp.

We are a research group within PALM Lab at Southeast University, led by Prof. Fan Liu.

Research → Publications

  • Perception & Understanding

    Learning representations from images, text and behavior to understand objects, interactions and user preferences.

  • Semantic Reasoning

    Interpreting semantic aspects and combining internal knowledge with retrieved evidence for grounded answers.

  • Autonomous Decision-Making

    Planning, tool use and action policies for agents and robots operating over multiple steps.
